Relay

Your coding agent, in your pocket.

Relay connects your iPhone to the coding agents running on your own Mac. Your repositories and API keys stay on your machine.

Privacy Policy Support

How It Works

Pair the iOS app with the Relay daemon on your Mac, choose a repository, send a task, and review diffs or test output from your phone.

Relay Pro

Relay Pro unlocks unlimited repositories, unlimited messages, custom endpoints, and full savings history through Apple subscriptions.

Local First

Relay routes requests from your Mac-side daemon. Source code, local credentials, and provider keys are not uploaded to Relay servers.
