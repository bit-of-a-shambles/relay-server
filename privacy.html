Relay

Privacy Policy

Effective July 8, 2026

Summary

Relay is designed to keep your work on your own devices. The iOS app connects to the Relay daemon running on your Mac. Your repositories, prompts, API keys, and agent output are not sent to Relay-operated servers.

Data Relay Handles

The app stores pairing details, repository settings, endpoint settings, and subscription entitlement state on your device. The Mac daemon stores local configuration on your Mac. Relay does not operate a cloud database for your code or messages.

Purchases

Relay Pro subscriptions are purchased through Apple. Apple processes payment information and manages subscription status according to Apple's policies. Relay receives StoreKit entitlement information needed to unlock paid features.

Bring Your Own Keys

If you configure custom agent or model endpoints, provider API keys remain on your Mac-side Relay daemon. They are used only to make the requests you initiate.

Diagnostics

Relay does not collect analytics or tracking data. If you contact support, you may choose to share screenshots, logs, or other diagnostic details.

Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue at github.com/bit-of-a-shambles/relay-server/issues.
