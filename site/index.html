Relay

Your coding agent, in your pocket.

Relay keeps your repositories, credentials, and session history on your Mac. Connect your iPhone to send work, inspect diffs, and check tests from anywhere. Your chosen model provider processes the requests your Mac sends to it.

How it works

A direct line to the work.

Relay keeps the Mac as the source of truth and gives you a focused remote control from your iPhone.

01 / PAIR

Connect your devices

Pair the iOS app with the Relay daemon running on your Mac over your local network or Tailscale.

02 / WORK

Choose a repository

Send a prompt to the coding agent working in your repository, with your local tools and credentials in place.

03 / REVIEW

Review from anywhere

Read the response, inspect diffs, and check test output before you approve the work.

Mac companion

Install the daemon first.

Relay routes requests from your Mac-side daemon. Source code, local credentials, and provider keys are not uploaded to Relay servers. The Homebrew package will be available at launch.

brew install bit-of-a-shambles/tap/relay
